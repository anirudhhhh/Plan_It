01 THE PLAN

Five apps. Twelve tabs.
One tired group chat.

From picking a destination to settling the last cab fare, here’s what we’re building into one place.

⌖

Group travel packs

Pick a ready-made route for a weekend or the week after mid-sems, with rides, stays and time for meals and connections built in.

↗

Tickets home

Compare train, bus and flight options for your dates and budget. See provider cancellation, change and refund terms up front.

⌂

Budget stays

Browse hostels, hotels and homestays with clearer prices, current photos and student feedback. Find women-only dorms where available.

⇄

Easy rides from Bihta

Plan cabs, shared rides and autos, including early or late runs to Patna Junction and the airport. See the fare before you commit.

÷

Split expenses

Track shared costs and see who owes whom for the ride, the stay and everything in between.

%

Student deals

Find group offers and budget picks through local operators and booking partners, with inclusions and fees spelled out.

03 MADE ON CAMPUS

It started with a ride out of Bihta.

In campus interviews, IIT Patna students told us about late cabs, misleading stay photos, missed connections and the scramble to find women-only dorms. PLAN-IT is a student project shaped by those stories.

Share your travel experience ↗
EARLY CAMPUS SURVEY · 23 RESPONSES
  • 65% struggled to reach Patna’s rail station or airport from Bihta at odd hours.
  • 61% reported being overcharged by local cab drivers.
  • 57% named trustworthy stays as a top planning challenge.
